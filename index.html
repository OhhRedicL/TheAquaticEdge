About Me

Leading from the stand, the street, and the front office.

Hi, I'm Jordan, founder of Laboy Aquatics Co. Originally from Phoenix, Arizona and now based in Southwest Florida, I've spent my career on pool decks, in ambulances, and running waterpark operations, and I've learned that great facilities are built by great leaders.

As a waterpark director, I'm responsible for the safety of thousands of guests and the growth of hundreds of seasonal staff. As a paramedic, I know what happens when a rescue turns into a medical emergency, and how much training matters in those first minutes. As an instructor, I get to turn those lessons into skills that guards and supervisors use every day.

My mission is simple: teach what I've learned, share honest insight into the aquatics industry, and inspire the next generation of leaders to take the next step.

Risk & Readiness Programs

Prepared before it happens. Supported after.

Training that gets your leaders ready for the worst moments, and helps your team recover and learn from them.

Before & during

Incident Command for Supervisors

Who does what in the first five minutes. Supervisors learn to take charge, assign roles, and keep the scene under control until EMS arrives.

Large events

Mass-Casualty & Reunification Drills

Realistic drills for large events and lost-child situations, so your team can manage multiple patients, crowds, and families with a clear plan.

After the event

Post-Incident Debrief Facilitation

A structured, no-blame review after real events, focused on learning. Your team leaves knowing what went well and what to sharpen next time.

Staff wellbeing

Critical Incident Stress Awareness

Supporting staff after a traumatic rescue. Leaders learn to recognize stress reactions, check in with their guards, and connect them to support.

Documentation

Incident Report Writing Workshop

Hands-on practice writing reports that are clear, objective, and factual. Guards and supervisors learn what to include, what to leave out, and how to describe what they saw and did, so every report holds up when it matters.
